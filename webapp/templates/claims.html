How claims are assessed

  • Supported / Contradicted: a precisely defined claim (measure, population, reporting date, number or benchmark) compared with the evidence. This is numerical support only; methodology approval is shown separately.
  • Needs clarification: the claim lacks a defined measure, population or period, or uses a word such as "minimal" that has no defined threshold.
  • Cannot be established: overall judgements ("better", "outperforms", "strong") for which FalconBridge has no approved criteria, measures SBA FOIA data does not contain, or other periods.
